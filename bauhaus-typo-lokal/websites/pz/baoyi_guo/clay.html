Let's make a candle holder with clay!🕯️

☕️STEP 1: Mix & Knead

 Knead stone clay together with coffee grounds until evenly combined.


☕️STEP 2: Roll It Flat

 Roll the mixture out into an even sheet.


☕️STEP 3: Cut the shapes

 Cut out a Christmas tree shape and a base.


☕️STEP 4: Add Holes & Assemble

 Poke small holes into the tree,
then attach it to the base.


☕️STEP 5: Add a Candle

 Place a candle inside. ✨ Done!

* things you will need:

stone clay
coffee grounds**

** P.S.

you can also replace coffee grounds with:

used tea leaves
fine sand
...

*** Don't recommend:

Sugar/salt
Fresh plant material
coarse particles
