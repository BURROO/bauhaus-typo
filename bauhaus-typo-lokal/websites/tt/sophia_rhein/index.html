drill

I drilled a hole in my wall. I went through layers, through different

materials, through time, which were exposed during drilling.

Why not turn this into a typographic web tool that picks up the

process? Here you can create your own abstract numbers,

materials and colors, and the direction you drill. You can convert

the numbers into a calendar or a clock. Have fun!
