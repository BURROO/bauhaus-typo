How to collect
the atmosphere of nature
in a bottle

🍊STEP 1: Clean and Dry the Plants

 Wash the plants you’ve chosen thoroughly (refer to the some juniper** in checklist for details),
then let them air-dry completely.


🍊STEP 2: Add the Leaves

 Pick the leaves and place them into a bottle.


🍊STEP 3: Clean the Orange Peel

 Wash the outer skin of the orange carefully.

🍊STEP 4: Separate the Fruit

 Cut the orange open and separate the pulp from the peel.


🍊STEP 5: Prepare the Orange Peel

 Remove the white pith from the peel, then cut the orange skin into strips.


🍊STEP 6: Add the Orange Peel

 Put the prepared orange peel strips into the bottle as well.


🍊STEP 7: Infuse with Alcohol

 Pour in alcohol and let the mixture sit for seven days. You’ll get a bottle of orange perfume with a natural, fresh scent!


🍊*STEP 8: Make an Orange Diffuser (Optional)

 You can also pour the liquid into a small bottle and insert diffuser sticks—-

now you have a homemade orange aroma diffuser ✨
* things you will need:

three oranges
some juniper**
a bottle
a knife
alcohol

** P.S.

The ingredient in the original tutorial is Platycladus orientalis leaves, which is a type of tree mainly found in East Asia.
Since it is not commonly found in Europe, it can be replaced with plants such as:

juniper
rosemary
spruce
