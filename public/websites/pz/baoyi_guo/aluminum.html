aluminum foil is more than just a tool!

🪩STEP 1: Prepare Your Pattern

 Draw the pattern you want to draw on a paper first.


🪩STEP 2: Scrape It Flat

 Use a ruler to smooth the aluminum foil.


🪩STEP 3: Draw the Pattern

 Tape the patterned paper onto the aluminum foil with masking tape.
Trace the design with a pencil, using light pressure to avoid puncturing the foil.


🪩STEP 4: Deepen the Pattern

 Take the paper off,use pencil and the end of a paint brush to deepen the pattern on aluminum foil.


🪩STEP 5: Cut the shape

 Use scissors to cut it out, and -- done!✨

* things you will need:

a piece of aluminum foil
a piece of paper
a paint brush
a soft pad
a pencil
a ruler
masking tape
scissors
