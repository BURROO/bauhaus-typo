Art by: Marcel Dzama

Marcel Dzama is an artist and not a graphic designer. His art "investigates human action and motivation, as well as the blurred relationship between the real and the subconscious. Drawing equally from folk vernacular as from art-historical and contemporary influences, Dzama’s work visualizes a universe of childhood fantasies and otherworldly fairy tales."
